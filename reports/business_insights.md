# RetailPulse BI – Business Sales Performance Analytics

# 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset to understand business sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product trends, and opportunities for improvement.

Tools Used: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Power BI

| Total Customers | 793 |
| Average Order Value | $458.61 |

# 3. Key Business Insights

# Category Performance
- Technology generated the highest revenue of $836,154.03.
- Office Supplies generated the highest category profit of $122,490.80.
- Furniture generated $741,999.80 in revenue but only $18,451.27 in profit.

# Regional Performance
- West generated the highest revenue of $725,457.82 and profit of $108,418.45.
- East was the second-highest region by revenue.
- Central generated $501,239.89 in revenue and $39,706.36 in profit, indicating an opportunity to improve profitability.

# Product Performance
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-revenue product, generating $61,599.82.
- Several technology products contributed significantly to overall revenue.

# Sub-Category Profitability
- Tables recorded a loss of $17,725.48.
- Bookcases recorded a loss of $3,472.56.
- Supplies recorded a loss of $1,189.10.
- Copiers, Phones and Accessories were among the strongest profit-generating sub-categories.

# 4. Business Recommendations

1. Improve Furniture Profitability: Review pricing, discounts and operating costs for Tables and Bookcases.
2. Strengthen Technology Sales: Maintain inventory and marketing support for high-performing technology products.
3. Improve Central Region Performance: Investigate product mix, pricing and discount practices in this region.
4. Optimize Discount Strategies: Analyze the relationship between discounts and profit to avoid unnecessary margin reduction.
5. Focus on High-Profit Products: Promote products and sub-categories that generate stronger profit contributions.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis indicates that the business generates strong overall revenue, but profitability varies across categories, regions and sub-categories. By improving loss-making product segments, optimizing discount strategies and strengthening high-performing categories, the business can identify opportunities to improve overall profitability.
